DBSCAN(eps=eps, min_samples=min_samples) — density-based clustering with explicit noise detection.

DBSCAN groups tracks that are densely packed together in feature space and marks tracks in low-density regions as noise (label -1) rather than forcing them into a cluster. Unlike K-Means, it does not need K specified up front and can find clusters of any shape. eps sets the neighborhood radius; min_samples sets how many neighbors a track needs within that radius to be a "core" track.
