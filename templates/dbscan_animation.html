What's happening
Each dot is a real track, positioned by its danceability (x) and energy (y). DBSCAN visits tracks one at a time: if a track has at least min_samples neighbors within ε (the pulsing ring), it's a core track and starts or joins a music community; neighbors that don't meet the density threshold themselves but sit near a core track become border tracks; tracks with too few neighbors anywhere become noise.
